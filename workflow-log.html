How Soumik judges a workflow

Five measures, and a log of what he shipped.

Measures

Measures

What it is, when it matters, how he moves it Show definitions

Human effort per task

What it is
The hours of people time a task needs from preparation through review, correction and rework.
When it matters
Any task repeated weekly or more often, where the manual baseline can be measured before the build starts.
How he moves it
He maps the task, times the manual baseline, removes steps first and only then automates what is left.
In his work
A 30% cut in manual outreach effort at CampK12 for a 400+ instructor workforce. For the Toronto founder, 20 to 30 hours a month of manual number-checking saved.

Output accuracy

What it is
The share of outputs that are correct and usable on real inputs without a person fixing them.
When it matters
Before a workflow touches customers, money or data that people rely on.
How he moves it
He tests with real inputs and edge cases, adds validation checks and keeps human review before any consequential action.
In his work
Python automation frameworks at Accenture for banking and healthcare clients cut post-deployment defects by 15%.

Turnaround time

What it is
The elapsed time from request to finished output, measured separately from effort.
When it matters
When people wait on the result, such as outreach, reporting, screening or onboarding.
How he moves it
He runs steps in parallel and removes handoffs that only pass work along.
In his work
Nine parallel B2B pipelines, built within 90 days, feed one dashboard tracking 2,314 prospects and $1.47M CAD of pipeline.

Adoption without help

What it is
The share of the intended users who run the workflow on their own without asking its builder for help.
When it matters
At handoff, and again after the first month of real use.
How he moves it
He writes a one-page operating guide, trains the team on their own tasks and makes the next step obvious at every point.
In his work
For the Toronto founder he wrote 9 SOPs so every workflow runs without depending on one person.

Cost per run

What it is
Model and tool spend per completed task, compared with the human effort it replaces.
When it matters
When volume grows and small per-run costs start to add up.
How he moves it
He uses the smallest model that holds quality, caches repeated steps and trims the input before it is sent.
In his work
The tools on the Shipped page cap input size, and the ones that handle people data strip contact details before any text reaches a model.
