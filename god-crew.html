Where it started

God Crew is a 24/7 biohazard and trauma scene cleanup company in Toronto. When Soumik joined, the business had one developer, one salesperson and a basic dashboard that recorded paying clients. There was no prospecting and no sales pipeline.

What he built

He split the growth work into nine separate pipeline systems. Each one finds prospects, enriches them and loads them into one live sales dashboard that the founder reads each morning.

Soumik and the founder shaped the dashboard together, and Soumik ran the whole build with Apollo, n8n and Claude Code.

He also wrote 9 SOPs, so every workflow runs without depending on one person.

What changed

The pipelines book 60+ qualified calls a month, and monthly recurring revenue is up roughly 30%.

The founder now sees all nine pipelines in one place and estimates it saves him 20 to 30 hours a month.

Beyond the pipelines

Soumik proposed a voice AI setup with ElevenLabs and Vapi in place of a 60 to 80 person sales hiring plan.

He onboarded the top 12 of 100+ influencer leads as affiliate partners, and ran a direct mail campaign to 1,579 households in the Greater Toronto Area.

He built the landing pages with Claude Code.

What it shows

Soumik starts from the manual work, splits it into systems a team can run, ships within a quarter and documents every workflow so it does not depend on him. That is the same way he would take a business function from 0 to 1 on a Claude delivery team.
