About

Soumik Chatterjee

Soumik Chatterjee is an AI Specialist in Kolkata with 9+ years across software engineering, client operations, education and founder support. He builds Claude workflows that teams can run on their own.

The name joins the end of Soumik with the cosmic scale of repeat work that AI can now take over. The .work ending marks it as his work portfolio.

Five things he goes by

  1. Everything is connected

    He keeps the larger picture in view, so small disagreements at work stay small.

  2. Less is more

    He removes what is not needed before he adds anything, in a workflow, a message or a day.

  3. Clarity first

    Most problems he has seen at work started with something not said plainly, so he says things plainly.

  4. Belief, then execution

    Self-belief only counts for him when the work follows it.

  5. A plan, and patience

    He works to a plan and gives it the time it needs to show results.
