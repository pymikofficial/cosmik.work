About Soumik

Why it is called cosmik.work.

With the right guardrails, almost every business process can now run as an AI workflow. Lead research, onboarding, reporting, follow ups, the weekly status update nobody enjoys writing. When nearly all of the repeat work in a company can be rebuilt that way, the scale of the change is cosmic.

That is the idea behind the name. I am Soumik Chatterjee, and for 9+ years I have taken work that people repeat by hand and turned it into systems that keep running. Today I do it with AI, and cosmik.work is where that work lives.

The spelling is a small play on my own name. Soumik ends in "mik", so cosmik shares that word with me.
