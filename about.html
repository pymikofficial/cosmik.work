About

Why cosmik.work

I am Soumik Chatterjee. I live in Kolkata and work remotely. For about nine years I have taken work that people repeat by hand and turned it into something that runs on its own. These days that means AI.

The name comes from two things. Soumik ends in mik, so cosmik. And when most of the repeat work in a company can be automated, the change is cosmic in scale.

Five things I go by.

  1. Everything is connected

    We are all made of the same cosmic material. Against something that size, most of what we quarrel over is small. I try to keep the larger picture in view, and the name cosmik.work comes from this.

  2. Less is more

    People overcomplicate things, partly because the brain likes to stay busy. Most problems get easier when you remove what is not needed. The same goes for a workflow, a message or a day.

  3. Clarity first

    Especially in communication. Almost every problem I have seen at work came from someone not saying the thing plainly. Say it plainly and most of the problem goes away.

  4. Belief, then execution

    Self belief can move mountains, but only when followed by doing the work. One without the other goes nowhere. They need each other.

  5. A plan, and patience

    Nothing worth having happens overnight. Everything needs a plan and a calculated approach, and then the time to let it work.
