Selected work

Solo build, 4 Nov 2026

TriageOps, an AI service desk for IT MSPs

An MSP service desk gets tickets from email, WhatsApp and client systems, and every one has to be read, prioritised and routed before anyone can help.

Claude reads each ticket in under six seconds, sets its priority and team, and drafts the reply from the client's own runbooks. Fixed rules keep ransomware at the top and send suspicious messages to a person.

  • Live in under an hour with Claude Code.
  • 8 sample tickets triaged correctly.
  • 896 automated tests on every push.
  • Forged and replayed requests blocked.

Built with the Claude API, Claude Code, Python, Supabase, React and Vercel.
