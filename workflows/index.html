Questions founders ask.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You tell me what the task looks like today, and I turn it into a workflow. You only need to approve the result and, if you want, give me access to the tools involved.

Where does it run, and who owns it?

It runs in your own accounts, in tools such as n8n or Make. You own the workflow, the data and the logins. If we stop working together, everything keeps running.

What will the tools cost me each month?

That depends on the tools and how much the workflow runs. Before we start, I tell you the likely monthly cost in plain numbers. Many small workflows cost very little to run.

What if it breaks, or does not save time?

I test every workflow on real examples before handover. If something is not working as agreed, I fix it. Optional monthly upkeep means I watch the workflows and repair them when a tool changes.

What happens to my data?

Your data stays in your own tools and accounts. I do not keep copies, and the AI model works through a key that belongs to you.

Why not use ChatGPT or my meeting tool directly?

You can, for one off tasks. What you pay for here is the part that runs without anyone remembering, connects to your tools, follows up until the task is done, and has someone who fixes it when it breaks.
